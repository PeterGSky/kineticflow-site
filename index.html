Questions people ask

Do I need a developer to connect WhatsApp?

No. Click Connect WhatsApp in Settings, log in with Facebook, choose your number and confirm it with a code. It takes about five minutes.

Can I keep using the WhatsApp Business app on my phone?

Not on the same number. When a number is connected to KineticFlow, its chats and WhatsApp calls move into KineticFlow, where your whole team can use them from any computer or phone browser. If you want to keep the app on your current number, use a UK number from us for KineticFlow instead.

Can I use my existing business number?

Yes. You can connect a number you already have, or use the UK number that comes with every paid plan. It's ready the day you sign up.

How are WhatsApp charges billed?

Meta charges for some WhatsApp messages, and for calls you make to customers, at its published rates. You add a card in Meta when you connect WhatsApp. Everything else is on your KineticFlow bill.

What does the free plan include, and what happens when a trial ends?

The free plan is for one user and stays free. Paid plans start with a one-month free trial; we take card details at the start and remind you 7 days and 1 day before it ends. Cancel before then and you won't pay anything.

Can I cancel at any time and take my data with me?

Yes. Cancel from Settings whenever you like. You can export everything to one Excel file, and if you close your account you have 30 days to change your mind before it's deleted.

Where is my data stored, and is KineticFlow UK GDPR compliant?

Your data is hosted in London. For the leads you manage in KineticFlow, you're in control and we act on your instructions under a data processing agreement, which comes with every account.

Does KineticFlow check numbers against the TPS?

Yes. Numbers registered with the Telephone Preference Service and the Corporate TPS are flagged before cold calls, and once a lead is marked "do not call" they stay that way.

How do the AI summaries work, and is my data used to train AI?

When a conversation changes, KineticFlow writes a short summary using Claude, an AI model from Anthropic. Summaries are labelled as AI-written and you can edit them. Your data is not used to train AI models.

Can I connect my Gmail or Outlook?

Yes, with the email add-on at £2 per mailbox a month. Emails to and from each lead appear on their timeline, and you can reply from KineticFlow.

Can I import leads from a spreadsheet or another CRM?

Yes. Upload an Excel or CSV file and match the columns. KineticFlow spots duplicates before they're added.

What happens to a lead once it's won?

Mark it as won and it moves to your customer records with its whole history: calls, chats, emails, notes and the AI summary.
